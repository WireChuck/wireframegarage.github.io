A note on the files

Built on trust

The model files here are sold on trust. I spend a painful amount of time designing them, and I share them with the community to make it easier for other owners to keep their Starquests on the road. These cars need the love.

If you buy a file, out of respect please keep the files, and anything you print from them, to yourself. Don't share, upload or resell them.

Thanks for keeping it fair. — Wireframe Garage

How it works

Model files

Print it yourself

Buy the file and we'll email it to you to print on your own machine. Every file comes with recommended material, orientation and settings.

Printed parts

We print and ship it

Parts are printed to order in high-grade materials built to last, whether they go inside the car, out in the sun or near engine heat. Most orders ship within a week.

Fitment

Check before you buy

Each listing shows the years and models it was designed for. Not sure it fits your car? Send a photo and we'll check.

Broken clip, cracked bezel, a trim piece nobody makes anymore — send photos or measurements and we'll quote a custom part or model file.
